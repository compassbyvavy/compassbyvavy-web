Description

July 2026 Girls Only Half Day Hockey Camp

2 hours a day on ice (10 hours), 2 hours a day off ice

Two available age groups: ages 5-9 and 10-14.  On ice groups are divided into like age and skill levels to ensure maximum learning and enjoyment for all players.

One group will be on the ice at a time.

DATES:                        July 20 -24 (Monday – Friday)
TIMES:                   9:00am – 1:00pm
LOCATION:                  Vic Johnston Arena, Streetsville, Mississauga

DETAILS:                     We offer : 5 days of 2 hours on-ice = 10 hours on-ice.  5 days of 2 hours off-ice including 30 minutes for changing and snacks, plus exercise and games. Players will not be bored!

Reasons to Attend

  • Primarily qualified/ experienced female instructors.
  • 10 hours of On-Ice training vs the average camp of 4-8 hours
  • 5  x 120 minute sessions provides a better training format vs average camp of 60 minutes
  • Both Technical Skating and Hockey Skills by experienced and qualified coaches and trainers  This combination is not found at other camps and will provide players with a distinct advantage.
  • Ability to ask a coach out to watch and speak with our instructors
  • High staff to player ratio
  • 10% EARLY BIRD DISCOUNT – if registration is completed by April 15, 2024. 
  • 10% “SIBLING” and “MULTI-CAMP” DISCOUNTS (please note that discounts cannot be combined)
    NOTE – Discounts are not available for Goalies

Methods of Payment

Once you have submitted the registration form, you will be emailed a confirmation along with payment instructions. Payment may be made by e-transfer or credit card.
