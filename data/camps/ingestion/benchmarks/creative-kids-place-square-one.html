Summer Camps – Square One in Mississauga, ON

 

  • Location

    Camps will be held at Sheridan College Hazel McCallion Campus located at 4180 Duke of York Blvd. in Mississauga across from the Square One mall.

  • Covid-19

    We will be following all guidelines by public health.

  • Camp Dates

    We will be running camps from June 29 to Aug. 28th, 2026.

  • Registration

    Registration for summer of 2026 will open on January 1st at 9:00 am. Please scroll down on this page to view all the information and camp schedule and outings before registering.

  • Camp Availability

    The number of spots available for each camp will be available during the registration process. You will not need an account to view the available spots.
