Our Summer Camps

Summer Camps (Ages 4–14)

Stay active, healthy, and have fun with a full day of gymnastics, games, arts & crafts, and many other exciting activities!

Gymnastics Mississauga Summer Camps provide children with an unforgettable experience filled with learning, movement, and adventure. Camp is a wonderful opportunity for participants to build confidence, develop new gymnastics skills, improve physical coordination and motor skills, and create lasting memories in a safe and supportive environment.

To ensure quality instruction and supervision, camp groups are limited to a maximum of 9 participants. Camps run daily from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM.

Each week campers will enjoy exciting new themes and special entertainment experiences such as Heroes in Action, Magical Disney, Wild Wonders, Spectacular Circus, Science in Motion, Stars Showcase, World Adventure, and many more fun surprises throughout the summer. Full-day campers will also enjoy special activities and a weekly pizza day.

Full-day campers will also enjoy special activities and a weekly pizza day.

Campers should wear comfortable sports clothing such as shorts, t-shirts, and running shoes. We also recommend bringing an extra set of clothes.

Please pack:

  • A lunch
  • At least 2 snacks
  • A water bottle

Half-Day Campers (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM)

Half-day campers will participate in gymnastics-based activities and should bring:

  • 1 snack
  • A water bottle

Please Note: Special shows, themed entertainment, and pizza days are available for full-day campers only.

Before and After Care

Starting  your day early ?  We have you covered !

Before Care  8:00am – 9:00am  $10.00

After Care 4:30pm – 5:30pm $10.00

Please Note Before and After Care are not gym classes

An annual Administration Membership Fee is applied to each student at any class or camp and includes a Gymnastics Ontario Membership Fee (July – June). See our policies for more details.

Week 10 August 31 - September 4, 2026 / World Adventure Camp

World Adventure

Get ready for a week of fun, fitness, and adventure! At World Adventure Gymnastics Camp, campers will explore exciting obstacle courses, tackle scavenger hunts, and compete in Olympic-style games—all while enjoying gymnastics challenges designed to build strength, balance, and confidence. Campers will be grouped as teams representing different countries, encouraging teamwork, sportsmanship, and friendly competition. Each day is packed with flips, jumps, climbs, and creative movement, combined with themed activities that make every moment an adventure. Join us at World Adventure Gymnastics Camp for a unique, action-packed experience where kids can learn gymnastics, stay active, and have a blast from start to finish!
