Nutty Scientists Canada we strive to provide the best STEAM learning experiences through fun and hands-on activities for our little scientists at our Summer Science Camp! 


Whether you like to explore science and hands-on experiment with different areas of science, or you are curious to know about STEAM, Nutty Summer Science Camp provides all opportunities to our little scientists to explore SCIENCE of for their own interest! ALL activities with hands-on science experiments in a group or individually, visual learning, information exchange (Nutty Facts). We cover different scientific topics every week along with specialized activities that align with science!


Our Summer Camp is the place where little scientists can explore every day different area of science learning with hands on, group discussion, visual learning, explore to understand, ask questions, and most importantly connect with science activities during the summer time & BE scientists themselves!
