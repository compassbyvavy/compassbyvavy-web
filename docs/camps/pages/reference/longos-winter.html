Before you go

Drop-off
A parent signs the child in at The Loft (the cooking room in the store) and signs a release and waiver. You can print and fill the waiver in advance.
Food
Kids make and eat their own snacks and lunch. No outside food or drinks. Bring a refillable water bottle. Baked treats can come home.
Allergies
Nuts are left out of kids' classes, but The Loft is not allergy- or nut-free. Contact the Loft Coordinator before camp about any allergy.
Age check
Children must be 6–12. A child below the age can be refused at drop-off.
