March Break Camp

Creative Kids Place · 3 Mississauga locations: Square One, Streetsville, Mississauga West

A full-day crafts, Lego and STEM camp where kids pick one theme for the week. Every day has outdoor play, plus a maple syrup festival trip on Wednesday, an animal show on Thursday and a carnival with pizza lunch on Friday.

Pick your camp

2. Days

Siblings: $20 off the 2nd and 3rd child when booked for the full week. All prices plus tax.

The week

Every theme gets the same special days.

MonTheme day + outdoor play
TueTheme day + outdoor play
WedTrip to the Maple Syrup Festival at Bronte Creek: maple tour, farm animals, wagon ride, pancake lunch
ThuHands on Exotics animal show: reptiles, birds and other exotic animals
FriCarnival with games and prizes; pizza and juice lunch included

Booking 2 days (Tue and Thu) means missing the trip and the carnival. Booking 3 days (Mon, Wed, Fri) means missing the animal show.

Before you go

Food
Pack a lunch and 2 snacks every day except Friday, when pizza and juice are included. Wednesday's lunch is pancakes at the festival.
Outside
Kids play outside every day, weather permitting, so dress them for March weather.
