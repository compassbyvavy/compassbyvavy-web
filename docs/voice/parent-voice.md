# Compass parent voice

Nov 16, 2026 · Vineeta Agarwal
Keep at `docs/voice/parent-voice.md`. Applies to every page Cursor builds: camps, classes, programs, venues, emails and admin text that parents might see.

## Who's talking

A Mississauga mom who has already called the camp, read the fine print and checked the dates, telling another parent what they need to know. Not a brochure, not a directory, not the camp's marketing.

## Rules

1. **Answer first.** What parents decide on goes at the top: ages, dates, price, hours, care, lunch. The story comes after, or not at all.
2. **Plain words, short sentences.** "Kids cook and eat their own lunch", not "participants engage in culinary experiences".
3. **Say what it costs in real terms.** "$45 a day", "$350 a week + tax", "Care: $40 a week each, or $8 a day". Never "from $X" without saying what X buys.
4. **Name the catch.** If booking 2 days means missing the field trip, say so. If the kitchen isn't nut-free, say so near the top. Parents trust a page that tells them the downside.
5. **Never invent.** A missing fact says "Ask the camp", never a guess or a filler sentence. Don't paraphrase a camp's claim into a promise ("safe", "best", "award-winning").
6. **Always say when we checked.** Spots, prices and dates carry "checked {date}". Spots are never "live".
7. **No hype.** No exclamation marks, "amazing", "unforgettable", "fun-filled", "world-class". Let the facts do it.
8. **Talk to the parent, about the child.** "Pack a lunch and 2 snacks", "Your child picks one theme for the week".
9. **Same words everywhere** (table below), so parents learn the site once.

## House style

| Thing | Write it as | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dates | Dec 21–23 · Mar 15–19, 2027 | 21/12, December 21st–23rd |
| Times | 9:00 am–4:00 pm · 10:00–1:00 (same half of day) | 0900, 9am-4pm |
| Prices | $45 a day · $350 a week + tax | $45/day, $350.00 per wk |
| Ages | Ages 4–12 · Grades 1–6 (ages 6–12) | 4yrs+, JK-6 alone |
| Care | Before & after care · Morning care · After care | Extended hours, BAC |
| Registration | Opens Nov 17, 1:00 pm · Open · Full · Closed | Coming soon, Book now! |
| Missing fact | Ask the camp (or Ask the studio, Ask the venue) | TBD, N/A, blank |
| Old fact | (2025–26) after the value | nothing |
| Buttons | Register · Save · Pick a day | Book Now!, Learn More |

## Section names (same on every listing)

Pick your camp (or Pick a class) · The week (camps with special days) · Before you go · Changes & refunds · How we checked

## Before and after

- Brochure: "An exciting, action-packed week of creativity and discovery awaits!" → Parent: "Kids pick one theme for the week: Lego, crafts, STEM or Stuff a Bear. Wednesday is a trip to the maple syrup festival."
- Directory: "Hours: 9-4. Extended care available." → Parent: "9:00 am–4:00 pm. Care from 7:30 and until 6:00, $40 a week each."
- Hidden catch: "Partial weeks available." → Parent: "You can book 2 or 3 days, but 2 days (Tue, Thu) misses the trip and the carnival."

## Classes and programs (weekly)

The same rules, with these answers first: ages · day and time · term dates and number of classes · price for the term (and per class) · trial or drop-in? · where. Section names: Pick a class · Before you go · Changes & refunds · How we checked.

## For Cursor

- Put labels, section names, button text and empty states in one copy file so they can be changed in one place.
- Templates must follow this guide. Provider text (e.g. "What makes your camp different") is shown as the provider wrote it, under their name, and is edited by Vineeta in admin, never rewritten by code or AI.
- When unsure, pick the shorter, plainer wording.
